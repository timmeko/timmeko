*Published: 2018 | The Washington Post*

Border cities are often treated as separate worlds. We wanted to flip that view and show how arbitrary the line can be—communities that look and feel connected, sliced apart by a border.

I loved this project because it let us lean into cinematic techniques—almost like designing a video game flyover—to capture that sense of movement across the line. We had to wrangle messy cross-border data and still make it smooth and lightweight on phones, which felt like a small miracle at the time.

This one sticks with me because it proved we could take a familiar, politically charged story and tell it in a way that was visual, unexpected, and deeply human.

**Role:** Graphics editor and technical lead  
**Highlights:**  
- Developed cinematic "flyover" experience for border story  
- Integrated complex cross-border datasets into smooth mobile experience  
- Created visually unexpected approach to politically sensitive subject  
- Balanced technical innovation with human storytelling focus
