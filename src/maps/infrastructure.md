# Mapping America's interconnected infrastructure

![Power grid](/img/raw/electric-1480-1440x890.jpg)

**[View project →](https://www.washingtonpost.com/graphics/national/maps-of-american-infrastrucure/)**  
*Published: December 2016 | The Washington Post*

A minimalist, multi-part tour of the systems that power, move, and connect the nation—depicting bridges, the electrical grid, pipelines, railroads, airports, and waterways as distinct layers of infrastructure.

**Highlights:**  
- Reported, wrote, and designed the entire project from concept to publication  
- Used minimalist cartographic design to strip away population bias and highlight structural form
